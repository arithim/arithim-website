THE PROBLEM

A distributor's range can run to thousands of technical products. Finding the right one for a customer means checking datasheets, dosing tables and regulatory documents, often in different places. Much of that knowledge sits with a few experienced people, and what the sales team learns in a meeting rarely makes it back into the CRM.

WHAT IT DOES
01
Product matching
Describe the customer's application and see which products in the range fit.
02
Dosing data
Dosing information for each product, next to the match.

The regulatory facts for each product, without hunting through separate documents.
