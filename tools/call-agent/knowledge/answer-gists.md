# Answer gists for callers

Question and answer-gist pairs, grouped by the agent that takes them. A gist says what a good answer contains; the agent finds its own words and keeps spoken turns short. Every statement of fact traces to the claims register (knowledge/claims.md), shown as "Claims:". Where a gist and the claims register differ, the register wins.

Roles were merged for the phone team: relationships (partners, press, vendors, research) sit with the front desk; billing and support sit with the customer desk; AP Clerk sits with consulting. Questions the register cannot answer yet are listed at the end under "Not answered yet (take a message)".

Question ids come from the question bank and are kept for traceability.
