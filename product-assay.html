FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Assay

What is Assay?

Assay is an Arithim service that tells you which AI models your business uses, flags the ones you have ruled out and gives you a dated report to show customers and supervisors.

Is an Assay report a certificate?

No. It is an attestation report that states its scope and date and says what we found in that scope. It does not claim to prove that nothing ruled out exists anywhere in your company.

Does Assay read our prompts or customer data?

No. It does not need to.

Which models does Assay flag?

The ones on the list you set, for example models named in rules that apply to you or in your customers' contracts.

Can Assay tell which model sits behind a supplier's product?

Often, though not always. Where we cannot confirm it, the report says so and marks it open, and suggests the question to put to the supplier.

Does a UK firm have to do this?

As of October 2026 we know of no UK rule that bans a specific AI model. Customers, suppliers and risk teams are asking the question, and some US rules reach UK suppliers. We will tell you if you do not need Assay.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

We scope it with you first, then quote.

Why is it called Assay?

An assay is a test of what something is made of. UK assay offices test and mark precious metals. We are not an assay office, and our report is not an official mark.
