FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Battery storage siting report

Where does the grid information come from, and how current is it?

From the registers that NESO and the network operators publish. Every figure carries its source and the date that source published it.

Does it cover Scotland and Wales?

Yes, for grid, designations, heritage, flood, farmland and peat. Land title information covers England and Wales only.

Can I change the assumptions?

Yes. Battery size, duration, cost and the treatment of grid reinforcement can be changed, and the report shows what moved as a result.

Does the report predict a connection offer or a planning decision?

No. It shows what has been published and labels cost and delay as assumptions. The network operator and the planning authority decide.

How long does it take, and how do I order?

Three working days from a complete order. Order by email today. Ordering through an API is in early access.
