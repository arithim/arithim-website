FOR EVERY SITE

What will I know about a site before I visit?

Every site opens into four answers you can take to a land agent, a network operator or your committee.

Can it connect?

The nearest substations, the headroom the network operator publishes, whether the site is likely to fit the current Gate 2 window, and who else already holds capacity there.

Will it get consent?

Schemes built, consented and refused nearby, how the local authority has decided similar applications, and the likely consent route for the size of your project.

Does it make money?

An indicative connection cost, the price per MWh the site needs to reach your hurdle rate, and how that moves when your assumptions change. For batteries, the break-even capex with and without the export credit.

Can we defend it?

A constraint checklist and the source behind every number. Any site can go into a feasibility report, a grid pack, an investment committee pack or an Excel model.

LIMITS

What does Site Finder not cover?

Site Finder screens sites. It does not replace a network operator's connection offer, planning advice or a site visit, and its connection costs are estimates for comparing sites. Where a network operator publishes no headroom, the site is marked unknown. Aerodrome and Ministry of Defence safeguarding is not assessed, so check it with the aerodrome operator, the MoD safeguarding team and, for wind, the NATS self-assessment maps before you commit. Coverage is Great Britain, and Site Finder does not show who owns a particular field.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions buyers ask about Site Finder

How does Site Finder help me find sites?

Tell it what you want to build and it ranks land across England, Scotland and Wales, with the grid, planning and economics behind each rank. Your team can spend survey money on sites that already look viable.

Which technologies and areas does it cover?

Solar, battery storage, solar with battery on a shared connection, and onshore wind, across Great Britain. Northern Ireland is not covered.

Does it replace a grid connection offer or planning advice?

No. It narrows where to look. Connection costs are indicative, so a network operator quote will differ, and planning advice, a site visit and aerodrome or Ministry of Defence checks still come before you commit.

Can it tell me whether a site can connect soon?

Where the network operator publishes a headroom figure, Site Finder shows it with its date, and where it does not, the site is marked unknown. It also shows who already holds capacity at the substation, and you can assume a Gate 2 offer and see how the ranking changes.

Can I test my own assumptions?

Yes. Change the technology, project size, revenue route, capex or hurdle rate, or mark a substation as full. The ranking updates and a card lists which sites moved and why.
