GRID PRE-SCREEN · GREAT BRITAIN

See which sites sit near grid headroom, and who already holds capacity there.

The grid pre-screen is an Arithim screen of one or more sites in Great Britain. It shows how close each site is to published grid headroom for the load or export you have in mind, and the main land and planning constraints that would stop it.

Request a screen →Early access
  1. Send the sites and the MW
  2. We find the nearest substations
  3. We check headroom and constraints
  4. You receive a short report

Today the grid screen is available for battery storage sites, inside the battery storage siting report. A standalone pre-screen is in early access. A demand-only mode for data centres and electrolysers is planned and is not available. Screening through an API is in early access for named customers, up to 5 sites per call.

WHAT YOU GET

What does the pre-screen show for each site?

Each site gets the same short set of answers, so a list of sites can be compared at a glance.

Nearest substations

The substations nearest to the site, and their distance.

Published headroom

Generation and demand headroom with its date and the operator's red, amber or green rating. The operator is named on every row.

Capacity already held

The registered projects already holding capacity at the node, and Gate 2 context.

Connection cost class

A rough cost class from labelled assumptions. It is not a network operator's budget estimate.

Land and planning constraints

The main constraints on the site.

Better sites nearby

A ranked list of better sites nearby, if there are any.

WHAT YOU SEND

What do I send?

  • One or more sites, as points or boundaries
  • The MW of import, export or both
  • For battery storage, the duration in hours

You receive a short PDF and the same results as data. The figures come from open public data published by NESO and the network operators, each with its source and date.

WHO IT IS FOR

Who is the grid pre-screen for?

Developers of battery storage, and the land teams and agents who source sites for them. The question is which of a set of sites is near a substation with room for the planned MW, and who is already in the queue there.

LIMITS

What does the pre-screen not do?

We state these on the page because they change what you can rely on.

  • Great Britain only.
  • Headroom is the network operator's published figure on its stated date. It is not a connection offer. Low voltage headroom is not published, and contracted queue positions are only partly public.
  • Connection cost is a class and a rough figure from labelled assumptions.
  • Data centres and electrolysers are planned and are not covered today. When that mode arrives it will not cover fibre, water or transmission connection dates.
FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Grid pre-screen

Is the headroom figure the same as the network operator's?

Yes. We show the figure the operator published, with its date and the operator's own rating, and we name the operator on every row.

Does it tell me who else is queuing at the substation?

It shows the projects on the NESO and network operator registers at that node, with their MW and status. Contract terms and the full queue are not public.

Can I send several sites at once?

Yes. The siting report takes one site, and the portfolio screen takes up to 1,000 sites from a spreadsheet, KML or GeoJSON. The screening API takes up to 5 sites per call in early access.

Does it cover data centres or electrolysers?

Not yet. That mode is planned. The grid screen exists today for battery storage.

Is the headroom live?

No. It is the figure the operator last published, with its date.
