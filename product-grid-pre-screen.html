GOOD TO KNOW

Good to know

  • The screen covers Great Britain.
  • Headroom is the figure the operator has published, on its stated date. It is not a connection offer.
  • Low voltage headroom is not published, and queue positions are only partly public.
  • Data centre and electrolyser screening is planned and is not available today.
FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Grid pre-screen

Is the headroom figure the same as the network operator's?

Yes. We show the figure the operator published, with its date and the operator's own rating, and we name the operator on every row.

Does it tell me who else is queuing at the substation?

It shows the projects on the public registers at that point, with their size and status. Contract terms and the full queue are not public.

Can I send several sites at once?

Yes. You can send a list of sites, and the screening API takes several sites per call in early access.

Does it cover data centres or electrolysers?

Not yet. That is planned. The grid screen is available today for battery storage.

Is the headroom live?

No. It is the figure the operator last published, with its date.
