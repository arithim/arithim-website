Questions

How is the Great Britain figure worked out?

No one publishes a live total for GB batteries. We add up the physical notifications of battery units in the Balancing Mechanism, the plan each unit sends to the system operator an hour ahead, from Elexon's data. It is an estimate: instructions after that hour change what actually happens, and smaller batteries outside the Balancing Mechanism are not counted.

Why do batteries charge in the middle of the day?

Where there is a lot of solar, power is cheapest around midday. Batteries charge then and discharge into the evening peak, when solar fades and demand rises. California shows the pattern clearly.

Why do batteries take in more than they give back?

Every charge and discharge loses some energy as heat, so over a day the energy charged runs a little above the energy discharged.
