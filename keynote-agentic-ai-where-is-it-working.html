In 2026, agentic AI works best on bounded, repetitive work where a person approves the risky steps, and most clearly in software engineering. These are the slides from the talk, with the speaker notes and sources underneath.

Slide by slide

  1. 01

    Agentic AI in 2026: where is it actually working?

    Speaker notes

    This talk draws on two Gartner reports: the Hype Cycle for Agentic AI, 2026, which profiles 33 technologies, and Predicts 2026: The New Era of Agentic Automation Begins.

  2. 02

    Bounded, repetitive work, with a person approving the risky steps.

    And most clearly in software engineering.

    Speaker notes

    Gartner's Hype Cycle says fully autonomous agents are not yet ready for most enterprise work. The agents that work today have a narrow job and a person in the approval path.

  3. 03

    An agent decides its own next step.

    • Chatbot Answers a prompt.
    • RPA Replays a fixed script across screens.
    • Agent Plans, calls tools, acts in other systems and adapts when something changes.
    Speaker notes

    Gartner warns about agent-washing, where assistants, chatbots and RPA products are relabelled as agents. A useful test is whether the product decides its own next step and acts in another system, or simply answers a prompt or replays a script.

  4. 04

    Very few processes will be handed to an agent alone.

    Gartner's wider term covers processes run by agents, conventional automation and people together.

    Speaker notes

    Predicts 2026 uses agentic automation for business processes run by a mix of AI agents, RPA and workflow tools, and people. That is closer to how most organisations will actually use agents.

  5. 05

    Adoption is early.

    • 17% of organisations have deployed AI agents
    • 42% expect to within 12 months
    • 22% expect to in the year after that
    Speaker notes

    These figures come from Gartner's 2026 CIO and Technology Executive Survey. Most early use automates workflows that already exist; very few organisations have redesigned a process around agents yet.

  6. 06

    They are arriving in the applications you already run.

    • <5% of enterprise applications had task-specific agents in 2025
    • 40% expected by the end of 2026
    Speaker notes

    Interest is running well ahead of deployment. Gartner reports that client inquiries about agentic technologies rose by more than 1,700% in 2025.

  7. 07

    Software engineering is the clearest success.

    • 90% of engineering leaders say AI has increased productivity
    • <25% the gain most of them report
    Speaker notes

    Coding agents now take on feature work, tests, code review and modernisation, with a developer reviewing the output. The figures come from Gartner's 2026 software engineering survey. It is the one area where Gartner sees significant growth.

  8. 08

    Five patterns that work today.

    • Bounded, repetitive tasks Clear steps and a clear right outcome
    • Domain agents in regulated work Claims, legal files and clinical coding, held to the domain's procedures
    • Agents that use screens IT support, web testing, data entry into systems with no API
    • Tightly controlled multi-agent set-ups With a checking step in the workflow
    • Document extraction Invoices, receipts and passports
    Speaker notes

    Enterprise AI assistants are the most widespread form, with more than half of the target market already using them. They mostly help people draft, analyse and report, and make fewer decisions on their own.

  9. 09

    Predictability is the main limit.

    • 45% average success rate for screen-based agents on a 50-step process
    Speaker notes

    Many agents can carry out specific tasks but lack the consistency to run unattended. Predicts 2026 puts screen-based agents well below RPA on the same kind of work, and they are slower and cost more per run.

  10. 10

    Cost, security and coordination.

    • Cost Varies run to run with context, tool calls, retries and reasoning time.
    • Security Prompt injection has no general fix. Borrowed logins break the audit trail.
    • Coordination One wrong output spreads down a chain of agents.
    Speaker notes

    Gartner recommends logging tokens, tool calls and cost for every step, capping steps and spend, and sending simple tasks to smaller models. Organisations piloting agents from several vendors struggle to coordinate them, and many agent products only work inside one vendor's environment.

  11. 11

    “If you agentify a garbage process, even automation will only amplify garbage.”

    Gartner, Predicts 2026

    Speaker notes

    Analyse the process before you automate it.

  12. 12

    Five steps before your first agent.

    1. Check whether simpler automation would do Rules-based workflow or RPA is often cheaper and more reliable for a fixed process
    2. Analyse the process first
    3. Keep a person approving high-risk actions Payments, sensitive data, changes to production
    4. Give each agent its own identity Short-lived, least-privilege credentials and a named human owner
    5. Stay independent of any one vendor MCP for tools, OpenTelemetry as a minimum for observability
    Speaker notes

    This is Gartner's advice across both reports. Never let an agent reuse an employee's login. Keep your prompts, workflows, policies and tests portable.

  13. 13

    Agents that work across the systems you already own.

    Each one has its own identity and limited access, logs every decision with its reasoning, and waits for a person to approve changes to production.

    Speaker notes

    Gartner expects that by 2030, 60% of organisations will adopt agent orchestration platforms from vendors outside their existing application suite. We build agents for that position, for example taking a security finding from any scanner through to a merged, verified fix.

Common questions

What is agentic AI?

Software that works towards a goal by planning steps, using tools and acting in other systems. A chatbot answers a prompt and RPA replays a fixed script; an agent decides its own next step.

Where are AI agents working in 2026?

Most clearly in software engineering. Elsewhere, on bounded and repetitive tasks, domain-specific work held to the domain's procedures, screen-based tasks on systems with no API, tightly controlled multi-agent workflows with a checking step, and data extraction from common documents.
