LIVE ENERGY DATA

Where can I see live solar, wind and battery data for Great Britain?

Four dashboards built on open data and refreshed every hour. Great Britain is covered in full; other grids appear as their operators allow their data on a commercial site.

Solar

Solar output now, by region, with a two-week forecast and a check of real output against modelled yield.

Wind

Wind output now, onshore and offshore, with NESO's 14-day forecast.

Batteries

What grid batteries are charging and discharging right now.

All renewables

Renewables by source and their share of generation, hour by hour.

How these dashboards work

Every hour a job fetches the latest figures from each grid operator and open data service, keeps only the sources whose licences allow display on a commercial site, and publishes them here. Each dashboard lists its sources and credit lines at the bottom.
