Case Studies / Compliance & Vendor Approval System
WORKFLOW COORDINATION

Compliance & Vendor Approval System

Structured workflows for approval, evidence collection, and stakeholder coordination, without flattening the human judgment that compliance depends on.

SECTOR
Workflow coordination
ENGAGEMENT
Systems design
FOCUS
Regulated approvals
THE CHALLENGE

Vendor approval and compliance workflows often fail because the system of record does not match the way decisions are actually made. Evidence lives in inboxes, ownership is unclear, and status is impossible to see at a glance.

THE APPROACH

We reframed the workflow around four things that were previously implicit: evidence, ownership, decision gates, and clear status visibility.

Evidence Ownership Decision gates Status visibility
THE OUTCOME

Reduced approval ambiguity and improved evidence handling across operational teams. The system created a more dependable path for approvals while leaving room for human judgment where it mattered.

"Approvals stopped being an argument about who saw what, and when."
