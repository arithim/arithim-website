Solar will work here. Shade is the main limit.

  • Trees to the south-west block the low winter sun. A building to the west shades the early evening sun in summer.
  • A south-east roof at 35 degrees is a fair direction and pitch, though not the best.
  • Shade takes about 15 per cent off the yield. Unshaded, the same system would give about 3,350 kWh a year.
