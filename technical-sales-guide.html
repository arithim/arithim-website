WHAT IT DOES

What can the Technical Sales Guide do for a distributor?

Technical answers sit with a few managers and in folders of PDFs. These are the six jobs the Guide takes off their desks, each shown with sample figures. You can pause any of the animations.

How do I find the right product for an application and condition?

Name the application and the condition, such as heat stress in broilers. Your rep sees which products fit best, from every supplier in your range, with the numbers needed to quote beside each one.

  • Dose, cost in use and landed cost beside each product, so the rep can quote on the spot.
  • Competitor equivalents, and Replace for when a supply line fails or a customer asks for another brand.

A rep at a Saudi broiler mill asks for a heat-stress solution and gets products from three principals, ranked.

Describe this animation

A rep types an application and condition. Three products from three principals appear in ranked order with dose, cost in use per tonne of feed and landed cost, and the sources are listed underneath. A competitor equivalent is found and Replace swaps Principal A for Principal C, saving $0.40 per tonne of feed. All figures are samples.

How do I answer a customer's technical question from our principals' documents?

Add the data sheets, safety data sheets and certificates your principals send. Reps ask in plain words and get the answer with the document and page it came from, so a technical manager can confirm it quickly.

  • The matching lines are highlighted on the page.
  • Certificates and documents carry expiry dates, and the owner is reminded before they lapse.
  • Details taken from a document, such as moisture or shelf life, go on a product page only after a person has approved them.

A technical manager uploads a principal's data sheet and asks for the maximum inclusion rate in layer feed. The answer points to page 3.

Describe this animation

A PDF data sheet is added and opens at page 1. A question about layer inclusion rates is typed. The answer appears with a citation to page 3 and the matching lines are highlighted. An expiry alert and an extraction waiting for review follow. All figures are samples.

Which of our products are registered where, and what expires next?

Each product and country has a registration record with the holder, the number, the expiry date and the document checklist. The owner is reminded before anything lapses.

  • Rules and key dates per country, with the products and customers each one affects.
  • A record per product and country: holder, number, expiry and documents.
  • An expiry reminder that becomes a renewal task.

A registration in Saudi Arabia is 60 days from expiry. The regulatory team gets an alert and a renewal task, and sales sees which customers buy the product.

Describe this animation

A table of registrations by product and country. One row for Saudi Arabia turns amber at 60 days. An alert appears, a renewal task is created with a checklist in which one document is missing, and three customers who buy the product are listed. All figures are samples.

How do I show a principal where its products win and lose?

Principals log in to a live view of their own products: what you proposed, won and lost, and why. The review packs you already produce for each principal by country become a screen the principal opens whenever it likes.

  • Wins, losses and recorded loss reasons by country and product.
  • Each principal sees its own products and nothing else.
  • The competitor is named where the rep recorded it.

A principal's regional manager opens the portal and sees wins, losses and loss reasons without asking for a report.

Describe this animation

A principal portal limited to one principal's products. Counts of proposals, wins and losses rise. Bars show results by country and reasons for lost deals. A note says a competitor was named on five of nine losses. All figures are samples.

How do I turn website visitors' questions into leads for the right team?

A product advisor on your website answers visitors from your catalogue and your principals' documents. When a visitor wants to talk to a person, it takes their details and sends the lead to the right team.

  • Visitors ask in their own language, including Arabic.
  • Leads arrive with the product, application and place already filled in.
  • You see what visitors ask for most, and the gaps in your range.

A visitor asks in Arabic about heat stress in broilers, agrees to be contacted, and becomes a lead routed to the Eastern Province team.

How do I produce a co-branded data sheet or application guide without starting from a blank page?

Choose a document type and the Guide drafts it from your catalogue and library, in your branding or with the principal's.

  • Branded and co-branded data sheets, application guides and comparison sheets.
  • Regulatory statements that use the registration details on file.
  • Draft content for your website.
  • Every draft goes to a person for review before it is used.

ALSO IN THE PRODUCT

What else helps the sales team day to day?

Around the six main jobs: a light CRM, training for new reps, a way to flag wrong answers, offline briefs, product data you can rely on and feed formulation tools.

Can we keep accounts and opportunities in the Guide without replacing our CRM?

Yes. The Guide has a light CRM for technical selling, with accounts, opportunities and territories. Import them from Dynamics 365 or Salesforce with ready-made settings, and keep the two in step through the API.

  • Sales roles see the territories they cover.
  • A lost deal needs a reason, and the reasons feed the principal review packs.

How do new reps learn our range and the rules that apply?

Short learning paths take a new rep through the range, the applications and the rules, one module at a time, and each module ends with a quiz.

What happens when an answer is wrong?

Anyone can flag an answer with one click and say what looks wrong. Flagged answers are reviewed each working day and corrected.

  • Each answer shows where it came from: a supplier source, an expert draft or a confirmed review.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about the Technical Sales Guide

How can my sales team answer technical questions without calling our technical manager?

The Technical Sales Guide lets a salesperson answer a customer's technical question without calling a specialist. It holds a catalogue of 466 products from 147 suppliers across 8 industries. Your rep asks by application and condition and sees the products ranked, with dose, cost in use and landed cost, competitor equivalents and substitutes, and the regulations and registrations that apply. It is a starting point for a technical person to confirm.

How is this different from Copilot or ChatGPT over our SharePoint?

A chat assistant over a document folder can quote a data sheet. The Guide adds what a folder does not hold: products from many suppliers and their competitors side by side, a ranking by performance for an application, cost in use and landed cost, and registrations per product and country. Answers show where they came from, and flagged answers are reviewed each working day.

Does it work with Copilot?

You can keep Copilot for chat over your own files. The Guide adds ranked, cross-supplier answers with cost and registrations, and other tools can request its rankings through the REST API. There is no Copilot connector yet.

Does it work in Arabic?

The interface is available in ten languages, including Arabic, which displays right to left. The website concierge takes questions in Arabic. Product and catalogue content is in English today.

Can a principal see our data?

A principal sees a live view of its own products only: where they were proposed, won and lost, and the reasons recorded. It does not see other principals or the rest of your pipeline. You decide who gets access.

Does it connect to Dynamics 365 or Salesforce?

You can import accounts and opportunities from Dynamics 365 and Salesforce using ready-made import settings. A REST API with scoped keys and signed webhooks lets you keep accounts and opportunities in step with a CRM or ERP, read the catalogue and request rankings.

How do I know the answers are right?

Every answer shows where it came from, such as a supplier source, an expert draft or a confirmed review. Anyone can flag an answer that looks wrong, and flagged answers are reviewed each working day and corrected. Registration expiries remind the person who owns them.
