WHAT IT DOES

What can the Technical Sales Guide do for a distributor?

Technical answers sit with a few managers and in folders of PDFs. Six jobs, each shown with sample figures. Press pause on any of them.

How do I find the right product for an application and condition?

Pick an application and a condition, such as heat stress in broilers. The Guide ranks products from every supplier in the catalogue for that condition, performance first, with the commercial numbers beside them.

  • Dose, cost in use and landed cost sit next to each product.
  • Competitor equivalents and Replace, for when a supply line fails or a customer asks for another brand.
  • Feed savings from enzyme matrix values, and a mycotoxin risk advisor.

Can I see which page of a data sheet an answer came from?

Upload the data sheets, safety data sheets and certificates your principals send. Answers cite the document and the page.

  • The page is highlighted, so a technical manager can check the answer against the sheet.
  • Certificates and documents carry expiry dates, and the owner gets an alert before they lapse.
  • Attributes such as moisture or shelf life are extracted into a review queue. Nothing reaches a product page until a person approves it.

How do I track product registrations and expiry dates by country?

Regulations, key dates and registrations sit against each product and country, with the holder and the document checklist. A reminder reaches the owner before an expiry.

  • Rules and key dates per country, with the products and customers each rule reaches.
  • A registration record per product and country: holder, number, expiry and documents.
  • An expiry reminder that creates a renewal task.

Principals log in to a live, permissioned view of their own products: what you proposed, won and lost, and why. The review packs you can already produce for each principal by country become a screen the principal opens at any time.

  • Wins, losses and recorded loss reasons by country and product.
  • Each principal sees its own products and nothing else.
  • The competitor is named where the rep recorded it.

SECURITY AND TRUST

Is the Technical Sales Guide secure enough for our principals’ data?

What is in place on 22 October 2026, and what is not yet. Your security team can ask for the questionnaire answers and the data processing agreement.

Is my data separate from other customers?

Each customer's data is separated inside the database by row-level security. It is enabled and enforced on every table that holds customer data. Arithim audited all 106 tables on 19 October 2026.

People are limited by role, and sales roles are limited by territory. There are six roles.

How do people sign in?

There are no passwords in the product. People sign in with an emailed link or a one-time code. Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app is available, and a company can require it for its administrators or for everyone. The default is optional.

Each company can set an idle timeout and a maximum session length. People can sign out of every device, and administrators can end a person’s sessions.

Can I see who did what?

Administrators can see an activity log of changes, imports, exports, sign-in events and security changes for their company, and export it. The application cannot edit or delete the log, and each entry is chained to the one before it, so tampering is detectable.

How do the API and webhooks protect data?

Administrators create API keys with scopes and an expiry. Keys are stored only as hashes, and a key stops working when its creator leaves, is demoted or is deactivated. Webhook calls are signed with HMAC-SHA256, sent only to https addresses, and blocked from reaching private network addresses.

How is the application itself secured?

Database queries use bound parameters, and user text is rendered as text. CSV exports neutralise spreadsheet formula injection. The only file uploads are CSV or text files for import, which are read and not stored.

Arithim reviewed the whole codebase for security on 19 October 2026 and fixed the findings in code. That was an internal review, not an independent penetration test. The codebase has an automated test suite of more than 200 test files, including tests for tenant isolation and access rules.

Where is my data hosted, and who processes it?

The application runs in Vercel's Frankfurt region (fra1) and the database is Supabase in Frankfurt (eu-central-1), as of 22 October 2026. Arithim acts as a processor for customer personal data, offers a data processing agreement, and gives 30 days’ notice of changes to this list.

Is my data sent to an AI provider?

AI features are optional and off until a customer turns them on. Arithim does not currently send customer data to an AI provider. Before it does, the provider is added to the published sub-processor list with 30 days' notice. When AI drafting is used, its output is a proposal that a person approves before anything is saved.

What happens to my data over time?

Companies can set retention periods, and the system deletes data past them each day. Administrators can export and erase a person’s data. On request, Arithim exports a company’s data.

What policies and incident plan do you have?

Arithim has written security, access control, data protection, incident response, continuity, secure development and supplier policies, available to customers under NDA. The incident response plan has a target of telling affected customers within 24 hours of confirming an incident. The plan has not yet been rehearsed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about the Technical Sales Guide

What is a technical sales guide for chemical and ingredient distributors?

It is a system that lets a salesperson answer a technical question from a customer without calling a specialist. The Technical Sales Guide holds a catalogue of 466 products from 147 suppliers across 8 industries. It ranks products for an application and condition, shows dose, cost in use and landed cost, lists competitor equivalents and substitutes, and tracks the regulations and registrations that apply.

How is the Technical Sales Guide different from Copilot or ChatGPT over our SharePoint?

A chat assistant over a document folder can quote a data sheet. The Guide adds structured data that a folder does not have: a catalogue that spans suppliers and their competitors, ranking by performance for an application, cost in use and landed cost, registrations per product and country, and a daily review of flagged answers. Every answer shows its sources.

Does it work with Copilot?

You can keep Copilot for chat over your own files. The Guide adds ranked, cross-supplier answers with cost and registrations, and other tools can request its rankings through the REST API. There is no Copilot connector yet.
