WHAT IT DOES

What can the Technical Sales Guide do for a distributor?

Technical answers sit with a few managers and in folders of PDFs. These are the six main jobs the Guide takes on, each shown with sample figures. You can pause any of the animations.

How do I find the right product for an application and condition?

Pick an application and a condition, such as heat stress in broilers. The Guide ranks products from every supplier in the catalogue for that condition, performance first, with the commercial numbers beside them.

  • Dose, cost in use and landed cost sit next to each product.
  • Competitor equivalents, and Replace for when a supply line fails or a customer asks for another brand.

A rep at a Saudi broiler mill asks for a heat-stress solution and gets products from three principals, ranked.

Describe this animation

A rep types an application and condition. Three products from three principals appear in ranked order with dose, cost in use per tonne of feed and landed cost, and the sources are listed underneath. A competitor equivalent is found and Replace swaps Principal A for Principal C, saving $0.40 per tonne of feed. All figures are samples.

How do I answer a customer's technical question from our principals' documents?

Upload the data sheets, safety data sheets and certificates your principals send. Ask in plain words and the answer comes back with the document and the page it came from, so a technical manager can check it.

  • The matching lines are highlighted on the page.
  • Certificates and documents carry expiry dates, and the owner gets an alert before they lapse.
  • Attributes such as moisture or shelf life go into a review queue, and nothing reaches a product page until a person approves it.

A technical manager uploads a principal's data sheet and asks for the maximum inclusion rate in layer feed. The answer points to page 3.

Describe this animation

A PDF data sheet is added and opens at page 1. A question about layer inclusion rates is typed. The answer appears with a citation to page 3 and the matching lines are highlighted. An expiry alert and an extraction waiting for review follow. All figures are samples.

How do I track product registrations and expiry dates by country?

Regulations, key dates and registrations sit against each product and country, with the holder and the document checklist. A reminder reaches the owner before an expiry.

  • Rules and key dates per country, with the products and customers each rule reaches.
  • A registration record per product and country: holder, number, expiry and documents.
  • An expiry reminder that creates a renewal task.

A registration in Saudi Arabia is 60 days from expiry. The regulatory team gets an alert and a renewal task, and sales sees which customers buy the product.

Describe this animation

A table of registrations by product and country. One row for Saudi Arabia turns amber at 60 days. An alert appears, a renewal task is created with a checklist in which one document is missing, and three customers who buy the product are listed. All figures are samples.

How do I show a principal where its products win and lose?

Principals log in to a live, permissioned view of their own products: what you proposed, won and lost, and why. The review packs you already produce for each principal by country become a screen the principal opens whenever it likes.

  • Wins, losses and recorded loss reasons by country and product.
  • Each principal sees its own products and nothing else.
  • The competitor is named where the rep recorded it.

A principal's regional manager opens the portal and sees wins, losses and loss reasons without asking for a report.

How do I turn website questions into routed leads?

A product advisor on your website answers visitors from your catalogue and your principals' documents, with sources. When a visitor wants a person, it takes their details and sends the lead to the right team.

  • Visitors ask in their own language, including Arabic.
  • Leads arrive with the product, application and place already filled in.
  • You see what visitors ask for most, and the gaps in your range.

  • Regulatory statements that quote the registration record.
  • Content drafts for your website, written from sourced facts.
  • Every draft goes to a person for review before it is used.

What else does the Technical Sales Guide include?

Tools for the sales team around the six main jobs: a light CRM, training for new reps, answer review, offline briefs, product data quality and formulation.

Can we keep accounts and opportunities in the Guide without replacing our CRM?

Yes. The Guide has a light CRM for technical selling, with accounts, opportunities and territories. Import them from Dynamics 365 or Salesforce with ready-made settings, and keep the two in step through the API.

  • Sales roles see the territories they cover.
  • A lost deal needs a reason, and the reasons feed the principal review packs.

What happens when an answer is wrong?

Anyone can flag an answer with one click and say what looks wrong. Flagged answers are reviewed each working day, and the fix goes in with that day's review.

  • Each rating shows its basis: a supplier source, an expert draft or a confirmed review.

SECURITY AND TRUST

Is the Technical Sales Guide secure enough for our principals' data?

What was in place on 22 October 2026. The security page has every answer in full, including what is still to come, and your security team can ask for the questionnaire answers and the data processing agreement.

Each customer's data is separate

Row-level security is enabled and enforced on every table that holds customer data. Arithim audited all 106 tables on 19 October 2026.

No passwords

People sign in with an emailed link or a one-time code. A company can require two-step sign-in for its administrators or for everyone.

A log that cannot be edited

Administrators see changes, imports, exports and sign-ins, and each entry is chained to the one before it, so tampering is detectable.

Hosted in Frankfurt

The application runs in Vercel's Frankfurt region and the database is Supabase in Frankfurt, as of 22 October 2026.

AI is off by default

Arithim does not currently send customer data to an AI provider. A provider would be added to the sub-processor list with 30 days' notice first.

Not yet certified

We are preparing for ISO/IEC 27001. Single sign-on and an independent penetration test are not available yet.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about the Technical Sales Guide

What is a technical sales guide for chemical and ingredient distributors?

It is a system that lets a salesperson answer a technical question from a customer without calling a specialist. The Technical Sales Guide holds a catalogue of 466 products from 147 suppliers across 8 industries. It ranks products for an application and condition, shows dose, cost in use and landed cost, lists competitor equivalents and substitutes, and tracks the regulations and registrations that apply.

How is the Technical Sales Guide different from Copilot or ChatGPT over our SharePoint?

A chat assistant over a document folder can quote a data sheet. The Guide adds structured data that a folder does not have: a catalogue that spans suppliers and their competitors, ranking by performance for an application, cost in use and landed cost, registrations per product and country, and a daily review of flagged answers. Every answer shows its sources.

Does it work with Copilot?

You can keep Copilot for chat over your own files. The Guide adds ranked, cross-supplier answers with cost and registrations, and other tools can request its rankings through the REST API. There is no Copilot connector yet.

Does the Technical Sales Guide work in Arabic?

The interface is available in ten languages, including Arabic, which displays right to left. The website concierge takes questions in Arabic. Product and catalogue content is in English today.

Can principals see our data in the principal portal?

A principal sees a live, permissioned view of its own products only: where they were proposed, won and lost, and the reasons recorded. It does not see other principals or the rest of your pipeline. You decide who gets access.

Does it connect to Dynamics 365 or Salesforce?

You can import accounts and opportunities from Dynamics 365 and Salesforce using ready-made import settings. A REST API with scoped keys and signed webhooks lets you keep accounts and opportunities in step with a CRM or ERP, read the catalogue and request rankings.

How do you keep the product data accurate?

Answers show their sources. Each rating shows its basis, such as a supplier source, an expert draft or a confirmed review. Anyone can flag an answer, and flagged answers are reviewed each working day and corrected. Regulatory dates and registration expiries raise alerts for the person who owns them.
