THE PROBLEM

Why do technical sales teams still call a specialist for every question?

A distributor’s range spans dozens of principals and competing products. The knowledge that sells it is spread across people, PDFs and spreadsheets.

01

Dozens of principals, one rep

A salesperson visiting a mill cannot know every product from every principal. The answers sit with a few technical managers and visiting experts, and the rep waits.

02

Principal reviews built by hand

Each principal wants to see where its products are proposed, won and lost, and why. Managers assemble that from spreadsheets before every review.

03

Registrations lapse quietly

One expired registration can stop sales of a product in a country. The expiry date sits in a file, and the document checklist sits in someone’s inbox.

04

Customers buy on cost in use

A price per kilogram hides what a nutritionist or formulator cares about: dose, what the product saves, and what it costs to land.

05

Data sheets live in folders

Principals send PDFs. Finding the page that answers a customer’s question means opening files one by one, and nobody notices when a certificate expires.

HOW IT WORKS

How does the Technical Sales Guide work?

STEP 1

Start from a catalogue that already exists

466 products from 147 suppliers across 8 industries, including animal nutrition, coatings, plastics and construction. Each has applications, conditions, grades and, where relevant, doses.

STEP 2

Add your principals’ documents

Upload data sheets, safety data sheets and certificates to the Library. Answers from your own files sit beside the catalogue, with page citations.

STEP 3

Ask from the field

Give an application and a condition. The Guide ranks products by performance and shows cost in use and landed cost beside them, with sources under every answer.

STEP 4

Correct it and keep it current

“Flag this answer” sends a wrong or unclear answer to a named expert, who fixes it every morning. Expiries and rule changes alert the people who own them.

FIND, MATCH AND REPLACE

How do I find the right product for an application and condition?

Pick an application and a condition, such as heat stress in broilers or road marking in cold weather. The Guide ranks products from every supplier in the catalogue for that condition, performance first, with the commercial numbers beside them.

  • Dose, cost in use and landed cost shown next to each product, so price is never the only number.
  • Competitor equivalents and Replace, for when a supply line fails or a customer asks for another brand.
  • Formulation tools: feed savings from enzyme matrix values, and a mycotoxin risk advisor.
  • Sources under every answer.

LIBRARY

Can I ask questions of my principals’ data sheets and see the page the answer came from?

Upload the technical data sheets, safety data sheets and certificates that your principals send. The Guide reads them, and answers cite the document and the page.

  • Answers point to the page, so a technical manager can check them in seconds.
  • Certificates and documents carry expiry dates, and the owner gets an alert before they lapse.
  • Attributes such as moisture or shelf life are extracted into a review queue. Nothing reaches a product page until a person approves it.

What the animation shows: a PDF data sheet is added and opens at page 1. A question about layer inclusion rates is typed. REGULATORY TRACKING

How do I track product registrations and expiry dates by country?

Regulations, key dates and registrations sit against each product and country, with the registration holder and the document checklist. A reminder reaches the owner well before an expiry.

  • Rules and key dates per country, with the products and customers each rule reaches.
  • Registration record per product and country: holder, number, expiry, documents.
  • Expiry reminders that create a renewal task.

PRINCIPAL PORTAL

How do I show a principal where its products win and lose?

Principals log in to a live, permissioned view of their own products: what you proposed, what you won, what you lost and why. Review packs, which you can already produce for each principal by country, become a screen the principal can open at any time.

  • Wins, losses and recorded loss reasons by country and product.
  • Each principal sees its own products and nothing else.
  • Competitor named where it was recorded, so the quarterly conversation starts from facts.

SECURITY AND TRUST

Is the Technical Sales Guide secure enough for our principals’ data?

This is what is in place today, stated plainly, with what is still to come listed after it. Your security team can ask for the questionnaire answers and the data processing agreement.

Each customer’s data is kept apart

Every customer’s data is tied to its own company. The database enforces this with row-level security on every table that holds customer data, and the rule applies even to the table owner. The application connects with a role that does not own the tables. Automated tests check the access rules and tenant handling.

Sign-in without passwords, with two-step checks

People sign in with an emailed link or one-time code, so the application stores no passwords. Authenticator-app two-step sign-in is available. Each company sets its policy: optional, required for administrators, or required for everyone. Sessions have idle and maximum-length limits.

Roles and territories

Roles inside a company control what each person sees and changes. Territory rules narrow it further. Administrators can view as another member of their own company for up to eight hours, and that action is recorded.

Encrypted in transit

Traffic to the application uses HTTPS and the connection to the database uses TLS. Storage encryption is provided by the hosting providers by default.

Audit trail you can read

The database keeps an append-only audit log of changes, imports, exports and decisions, and sign-in events are recorded. Administrators can see the activity log for their own company. Arithim can provide an extract on request.

Data export and exit

Accounts, pipeline and knowledge can be exported as CSV from the application, and the data processing agreement provides for a full export when a contract ends. A data processing agreement is available on request.

AI use is off until you turn it on

AI drafting is off for every customer unless the licence enables it. When it is on, text you submit goes to Anthropic, which processes in the United States by default. Document and visitor text is treated as data and never as instructions.

Incidents and monitoring

There is a written incident response plan. The target is to tell a customer within 24 hours of confirming a personal data breach that affects its data. Errors and uptime are monitored in our own EU database, with identifiers scrubbed from error messages.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about the Technical Sales Guide

What is a technical sales guide for chemical and ingredient distributors?

It is a system that lets a salesperson answer a technical question from a customer without calling a specialist. The Technical Sales Guide holds a catalogue of 466 products from 147 suppliers across 8 industries. It ranks products for an application and condition, shows dose, cost in use and landed cost, lists competitor equivalents and substitutes, and tracks the regulations and registrations that apply.

How is the Technical Sales Guide different from Copilot or ChatGPT over our SharePoint?

A chat assistant over a document folder can quote a data sheet. The Guide adds structured data that a folder does not have: a catalogue that spans suppliers and their competitors, ranking by performance for an application, cost in use and landed cost, registrations per product and country, and a named expert who fixes flagged answers every morning. Every answer shows its sources.

Does the Technical Sales Guide work in Arabic?

The interface is available in ten languages, including Arabic, which displays right to left. The website concierge can take questions in Arabic. Product and catalogue content is in English today.

Where is our data hosted, and is it used to train AI models?

The database is hosted in the EU and the application runs from Frankfurt. Each customer's data is isolated by row-level security. AI drafting is off unless your company turns it on; when it is on, the text you submit goes to Anthropic. Before AI drafting is enabled for a customer, Arithim checks the provider's current terms on training and confirms the position in writing. See the security section on this page for the full list of sub-processors.

Can principals see our data in the principal portal?

A principal sees a live, permissioned view of its own products only: where they were proposed, won and lost, and the reasons recorded. It does not see other principals or the rest of your pipeline. You decide who gets access.

Does it connect to Dynamics 365 or Salesforce?

You can import accounts and opportunities from Dynamics 365 and Salesforce using ready-made import settings. A REST API with scoped keys and signed webhooks lets you keep accounts and opportunities in step with a CRM or ERP, read the catalogue and request rankings.

How do you keep the product data accurate?

Answers show their sources, so a reader can check them. Anyone can use “Flag this answer”, and a named expert reviews the flags and fixes them every morning. Regulatory dates and registration expiries raise alerts for the person who owns them.

Is Arithim certified to ISO 27001 or SOC 2?

No. Arithim is running a readiness programme for ISO/IEC 27001 and holds no certification today. We do not claim SOC 2. The security section lists the controls that are in place, the ones still being built, and the sub-processors we use, so your team can judge them directly.
