Unrejectable

An AI tool that pressure-tests a job application before a human ever sees it — reading a CV and application the way a screener does, then telling the candidate exactly what to fix to get past the first cut.

TYPE
Owned product
DOMAIN
Careers & recruitment AI
STATUS
Live
THE PROBLEM

Most applications are rejected at automated or first-pass screening, and the candidate never learns why. Good people get filtered out for fixable reasons — weak framing, missing keywords, unclear evidence — and keep repeating the same mistakes across every application.

HOW IT WORKS

The candidate drops in their CV and the role they're targeting. Unrejectable analyses it against how screening actually works, pinpoints the weaknesses that get applications binned, and rewrites the framing — so what lands in front of a recruiter is hard to say no to.

DRAFT
ANALYSE

WHY IT MATTERS

Unrejectable moves the feedback loop to before the rejection, where it can still change the outcome. It's the sister product to Find Me A Job: one finds the right roles, the other makes the application land — the same practical-AI thesis applied end to end.

"Rejection is usually a framing problem, not a talent problem."
