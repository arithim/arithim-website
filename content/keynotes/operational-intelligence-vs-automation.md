--- title
kicker: Keynote · Operational intelligence
# Operational intelligence vs ==automation==
notes: Both matter, and they solve different problems. Confusing the two is how AI projects end up impressive in a demo and useless in production.

--- cards
kicker: Two different jobs
## Each one solves a different problem.
- Automation | Moves a task from manual execution to structured execution.
- Operational intelligence | Improves how information, judgement and action move through a business.
notes: Automation changes how one task gets done. Operational intelligence changes how well people see, decide and act across many tasks.

--- statement
kicker: Automation
## Same input, same rule, ==same result==.
Use it when a repeatable step can be handled the same way every time.
notes: Automation is dependable because the step does not change. When the right answer depends on context, a fixed rule will eventually get it wrong.

--- statement
kicker: Operational intelligence
## When the right action depends on ==what else is true==.
Use it when teams need visibility, context, evidence or decision support in a changing environment.
notes: Here the value comes from bringing the right information to the person deciding, at the moment they decide.

--- quote
> Automation for dependable execution. Intelligence for coordination and control.
cite: Arithim
notes: Most operations need both. The question is which one a given problem needs.

--- statement
kicker: The discipline
## Strong systems usually contain both.
The skill is knowing which one a problem needs, and not paying for the wrong one.
notes: Before buying either, describe the problem. If the step is fixed, automate it. If the right action depends on changing context, start with intelligence.
