--- title
kicker: Keynote · AI implementation
# What most businesses ==actually need== from AI
notes: Most organisations do not need a louder AI strategy. They need a clearer operating model for where AI can reduce friction and support better decisions.

--- statement
kicker: Where to start
## Start with ordinary questions about the work.
notes: Useful AI work rarely starts with a model. It starts with the places where the business already loses time.

--- list
kicker: Four questions
## Where does the work get ==stuck==?
- Where does information stall?
- Where do teams repeat low-value work?
- Where does reporting arrive too late?
- Where does knowledge live outside the systems people use every day?
notes: Each answer points at a place where AI might reduce friction. Ask them of one team before you ask them of the whole organisation.

--- list numbered
kicker: The path
## Smaller and more disciplined than the hype.
- Map the workflow
- Find the decision points
- Improve the data surface
- Add automation or intelligence | Only where it changes the work
notes: The implementation path is often smaller than the hype suggests. Automation or intelligence comes last, once the workflow and the data are clear.

--- quote
> AI becomes valuable when it is part of a system people can trust, operate, and improve.
cite: Arithim
notes: The model is one part. The people who run it, the data it reads and the way it gets fixed decide whether it lasts.

--- statement
kicker: The test
## Will it survive a ==normal Tuesday==?
The system around the model matters more than the demo.
notes: A demo shows what a model can do once. A system people can run, trust and improve is what keeps working on an ordinary day.
