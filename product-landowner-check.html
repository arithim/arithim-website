FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Landowner check

What is a landowner check?

A one page report on your land, the nearest grid point, the developer who approached you and any pressure terms in their letter, with questions for an adviser.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

£195 a report, excluding VAT. We deliver it within three working days of a complete order.

Does it tell me whether to sign?

No. It gives you facts and questions to take to a solicitor or land agent. It is not legal or financial advice.

What do I need to send?

A postcode or a map pin, the developer's name and, if you have it, their letter.

Does it cover Scotland and Wales?

It covers Great Britain. Northern Ireland is not covered.

What if the developer is a company I cannot find?

We say what we could and could not find about them. We do not guess.

How do I order?

Press Order and your email app opens a message with the details we need. We confirm the order by email.
