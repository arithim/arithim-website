FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Portfolio screen

What is a portfolio screen?

A check of every claim in a seller's list of sites against the public record, with each claim marked confirmed, contradicted, not verifiable or seller-supplied.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

£7,500 for up to 50 sites and £120 for each extra site, excluding VAT, delivered in five working days.

What do I need to send?

The seller's site list as a spreadsheet, KML or GeoJSON, and the number of sites.

What does not verifiable mean?

The public record has nothing we can check the claim against. We say so and do not guess.

Can I share it with my lender or committee?

Yes. It comes as a PDF with sources and licences, and an Excel workbook.

Is it a substitute for diligence?

No. It is a fast independent check on the seller's claims. Legal, technical and commercial diligence still apply.

How do I order?

Press Order and your email app opens a message with the details we need. We confirm the order by email.
