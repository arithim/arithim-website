TENDER FEED · PLANNED

Get new UK public tenders that fit your company, with the reasons for each match.

The tender feed is a planned Arithim service. You describe a company once and then receive new UK public sector tenders that fit it, each with a fit score, the reasons for the score, the deadline and what the buyer organisation has bought before.

WHAT YOU GET

What will the feed return?

These are the planned contents.

New matching tenders

Title, buyer, value, deadline and a link to the notice.

A fit score with reasons

So you can check the match yourself, for example a matching code, region or value range.

Alerts

Webhooks for a new match, a deadline within seven days and an amendment to a notice.

Buyer history

What that buyer has awarded before, taken from award notices.

Profiles for many clients

Several profiles per account, for consultancies that serve many clients.

WHAT YOU SEND

What will I send?

  • A company profile: industry codes, regions, a value range, keywords and exclusions
  • Several profiles per account, if you serve many clients

The feed is planned to use notices from UK government tender notice services.

WHO IT IS FOR

Who is the tender feed for?

Bid consultancies, agencies and platforms that serve many supplier clients, and suppliers who want a feed they can plug into their own tools. The question is which new public contracts are worth bidding for, and who has won this buyer's work before.

LIMITS

What will the feed not do?

We state these on the page because they change what you can rely on.

  • The first release is planned to use UK government tender notice services only.
  • Notices from Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and the EU may come later and are not promised.
  • It does not provide bid documents, write bids or submit them to buyers.
  • The fit score is automated and will sometimes be wrong.
  • Early signals before a formal notice appear only where buyers publish them.
  • Buyer contact details are limited to what appears in notices.
FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Tender feed

Which tenders does it cover?

Early access is planned to use notices from the UK government's published tender notice services. We have not published a wider coverage statement.

Does it write or submit bids?

No. It finds and ranks opportunities and shows the buyer's history. Bidding stays with you.

How do I know why a tender matched?

Every match comes with the reasons for its score, for example a matching code, region or value range, so you can check it yourself.

Is it available now?

No. It is planned. Early access will start with a small group of named users.
