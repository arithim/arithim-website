FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Site feasibility report

What is a site feasibility report?

A report on one site in Great Britain that gives a verdict with reasons and joins the grid, planning, land and economics for that site, with sources.

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

£1,950 a site, excluding VAT, delivered in three working days. The solar and battery hybrid analysis is £500 on top.

How is this different from the Battery storage siting report?

The Battery storage siting report is built for battery sites, to decide whether one is worth pursuing and what would end it. The site feasibility report covers solar, battery or wind, with planning, economics, contacts and sources.

What do I need to send?

The location (a point, a drawn boundary, or a KML or shapefile), the technology and the project size in MW.

What is the hybrid analysis?

Hybrid economics for solar and battery on one connection, with clipping and dispatch, compared with solar alone. It needs the size of both.

Can I share it with a lender or committee?

Yes. The report carries sources and licences so others can check it. It is a screening aid and not a substitute for advice from the bodies that decide.
