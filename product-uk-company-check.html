UK COMPANY CHECK · PLANNED

Check who is behind a UK company, with a dated source for every fact.

The UK company check is a planned Arithim service for know your business (KYB) checks. You send a company number or name and get back a summary: whether the company exists and is active, who controls it as far as the UK register shows, and anything that should make you look closer, with the source and retrieval date for each item.

  1. Send a company number or name
  2. We read the UK register
  3. We flag what needs a closer look
  4. You get a summary with sources

This product is planned and is not yet available. Register your interest and we will tell you when early access opens. Early access will cover UK registered companies only, and only the checks that are built and licensed at the time.

WHAT YOU GET

What will the check return?

These are the planned contents. Each item will carry its source and the date we retrieved it.

Company status

Whether the company exists and is active, its incorporation date, registered office, SIC codes and filing history, from Companies House.

Corporate controllers

The chain of corporate persons with significant control recorded with Companies House. Individual owners are not shown.

Flags with plain reasons

Anything that needs a closer look, such as an owner registered abroad or overdue accounts. The check returns clear or review. It does not tell you to accept or reject a customer.

Evidence for every item

The source and the date we retrieved it.

Energy holdings

For energy counterparties, the renewable and storage projects the company's group holds or has applied for. This part is built today.

Checks still to come

Insolvency notices, sanctions screening and ongoing monitoring with alerts are planned and are not in early access yet.

WHAT YOU SEND

What will I send?

  • A UK company number or company name

Company data comes from Companies House, the UK's official register of companies. Names of individual officers and owners are personal data, and we will not offer them until we have a lawful basis and a retention rule.

WHO IT IS FOR

Who is the UK company check for?

Accountancy firms, lenders, marketplaces and B2B platforms that onboard UK business customers or suppliers, and energy developers checking a landowner company or an offtaker before they sign an option or a power purchase agreement.

LIMITS

What will the check not do?

We state these on the page because they change what you can rely on.

  • UK registered companies only. Ownership chains that leave the UK are not followed, and the chain stops where the UK register stops.
  • Ownership comes from the people with significant control register, which companies complete themselves. We report what the register says and do not verify it independently.
  • No credit scores or credit limits.
  • No politically exposed persons screening and no adverse media.
  • It does not verify the identity of directors.
  • It is a screening aid. It does not decide whether you meet your own legal duties.
FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about UK company check

What is a KYB check?

KYB means know your business. It is the check that a company is real, who stands behind it, and whether anything should make you look closer before you deal with it. This service supports that check. It does not make you compliant on its own.

Is the UK company check available now?

No. It is planned. Early access will start with a small group of named users.

Does it work for companies outside the UK?

No. Early access covers companies registered in the UK only.

Does it tell us whether to accept a customer?

No. It returns flags, reasons and evidence for your own team to decide.

Can I see where each fact came from?

Yes. Every item will list its source and the date we retrieved it.

Will it show me who owns the company?

It will show the chain of corporate owners recorded with Companies House. Individual owners are not shown in the current design.
