GOOD TO KNOW

Good to know

  • UK registered companies only. Ownership chains that leave the UK stop where the UK register stops.
  • Ownership comes from information that companies file about themselves. We report what the register says and do not verify it.
  • No credit scores, politically exposed persons screening or adverse media.
  • It is a screening aid. It does not decide whether you meet your own legal duties.
FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about UK company check

What is a KYB check?

KYB means know your business: checking that a company is real, who stands behind it and whether anything should make you look closer before you deal with it. This service supports that check. It does not make you compliant on its own.

Is the UK company check available now?

No. It is planned. Early access will start with a small group of named users.

Does it work for companies outside the UK?

No. Early access covers companies registered in the UK only.

Does it tell us whether to accept a customer?

No. It returns flags, reasons and evidence for your own team to decide.

Can I see where each fact came from?

Yes. Every item will show its source and the date we retrieved it.

Will it show me who owns the company?

It will show the chain of corporate owners recorded with Companies House. Individual owners are not shown in the current design.
