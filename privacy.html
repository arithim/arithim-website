PRIVACY

Privacy policy

LAST UPDATED · 21 OCTOBER 2026

Who we are

Arithim Ltd is an applied AI company based in London, United Kingdom. It builds software products and works with clients on AI projects. This policy explains what limited information we collect through this website and how we handle it.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us: the details you enter in the contact form, and any messages you send to hello@arithim.com or research@arithim.com. The site itself uses no advertising trackers. The contact and demo request forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated submissions and Resend to deliver your message to our inbox.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss a potential engagement or research collaboration. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Calls and chats with our AI assistants

The Call us button on this site lets you speak or type to Arithim's AI assistants. They are AI, not people, and you are told so before the call starts. The service is for people aged 18 or over, and for business enquiries. Please do not use it to share sensitive personal information such as health details. The assistants do not make any decision about you that has a legal or similarly significant effect. They answer questions, take messages and pass them to a person, who replies within one working day.

What we collect from calls and chats

Your voice is processed live so that it can be turned into text. We ask the voice provider not to keep the audio. We keep the transcript of the conversation, the details you choose to give (such as your name, organisation, email address or phone number, and what you want to talk about), a short summary, and technical data such as your IP address, which we store only as a salted hash and use to prevent abuse and limit repeated use.

Why we use it

We use this information to answer your enquiry, to pass your message to Tarun, to keep the service secure and within its usage limits, and to improve how the assistants answer. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in answering enquiries and running the service safely. Where you ask us to take steps towards an engagement, we also use it to do so at your request.

Who processes it for us

ElevenLabs processes your voice and runs the voice conversation. Anthropic provides the AI model that writes the assistants' replies. Cloudflare hosts this site and stores the call records. Resend sends the internal notification email to us. Each acts as a processor on our instructions, under its data processing terms. We do not sell this information and do not use it for marketing.

Where your data is processed

These providers process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UK, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or on the UK extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, as set out in each provider's data processing terms.

How long we keep it

ElevenLabs keeps conversation data for no more than 30 days and does not store the audio. Our own records of calls and chats are deleted after 90 days. We keep a message for longer only where it becomes an ongoing enquiry or engagement, and then under the same terms as the rest of our correspondence with you.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the information you have shared with us. This includes anything you told our AI assistants. You can also object to our use of your information, ask us to restrict it, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. To make a request, email hello@arithim.com, quoting the reference the assistant gave you if you have one, and we will respond promptly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@arithim.com.
