Questions

Where do the Great Britain figures come from?

Sheffield Solar's PV_Live service estimates all of Great Britain's solar output every half hour, scaling up readings from a sample of metered systems. The forecast is NESO's embedded solar forecast. Both are published under open licences.

Why is output so far below installed capacity?

Panels reach their rated output only in bright, cool sunshine with the sun high and square on. Cloud, season, time of day and the angle each array faces all pull it down, which is why the regional map shows capacity factor: the share of what the panels could make running flat out.

How up to date is this page?

The data is fetched every hour. Each grid publishes on its own schedule, so the latest reading for one may be a few minutes old and for another an hour or two.
