SOLAR SUITABILITY API · PLANNED

Find out if solar is likely to work at an address before you pay for a survey.

The solar suitability API is a planned Arithim service. You send an address or a site and get back a plain verdict on whether solar is likely to work there, the reasons, the planning and environmental constraints found, and an estimated annual yield, with the source for each item.

Register your interest →Planned
  1. Send an address, UPRN, coordinates or boundary
  2. We check constraints and yield
  3. We give a verdict with reasons
  4. You decide whether to book a survey

This product is planned and is not yet available. Register your interest and we will tell you when early access opens. Early access will cover the layers that are built and licensed at the time. We have not confirmed geographic coverage, and we will state it before anyone relies on it.

WHAT YOU GET

What will the check return?

These are the planned contents of a response.

A plain verdict

Suitable, likely suitable, constrained or unlikely, with the reasons in plain words.

Constraints found

Listed building status, conservation area, flood zone and planning designations.

Grid headroom

Only where the network operator publishes it.

Estimated annual yield

In kWh, for a stated system size.

An evidence list

The source and date for each item.

Batch screening

A batch of addresses for portfolio screening.

WHAT YOU SEND

What will I send?

  • An address, a UPRN or coordinates for a building
  • For a larger site, a boundary polygon and an intended system size
  • A batch of addresses, for portfolio screening

Each item in a response will name its source and date. We will list the sources once their licence terms are confirmed.

WHO IT IS FOR

Who is the solar suitability check for?

Solar installers qualifying leads before paying for a survey, lenders and landlords screening a portfolio of buildings, and developers doing an early check on a site. The question is whether the building or site is worth a survey or a bid, and what could stop it.

LIMITS

What will the check not do?

We state these on the page because they change what you can rely on.

  • It is not a design tool. There is no panel layout and there are no engineering drawings.
  • It is not a quote and gives no payback or savings figures.
  • It does not replace a site survey or any installer certification.
  • Roof accuracy is not yet measured.
  • Grid headroom appears only where an operator publishes it, and that is uneven.
  • Planning history differs between councils, so it will be incomplete.
  • Shading from trees and neighbouring buildings is approximate.
  • Geographic coverage is not yet confirmed.
FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Solar suitability check

Does it design the system for me?

No. It tells you whether solar is likely to work and what could get in the way. It does not lay out panels or produce drawings.

Does it replace a site survey?

No. It helps you decide whether a survey is worth booking.

Can it tell me if I will get planning permission?

No. It flags known constraints such as a conservation area or listed status. The council decides permission.

Is it available now?

No. It is planned. Early access will start with a small group of named users.
