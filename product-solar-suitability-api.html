GOOD TO KNOW

Good to know

  • It is not a design tool. There is no panel layout and there are no engineering drawings.
  • It is not a quote and gives no payback or savings figures.
  • It does not replace a site survey.
  • Grid headroom appears only where an operator publishes it.
  • Planning history differs between councils and will be incomplete.
FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions about Solar suitability check

Does it design the system for me?

No. It tells you whether solar is likely to work and what could get in the way. It does not lay out panels or produce drawings.

Does it replace a site survey?

No. It helps you decide whether a survey is worth booking.

Can it tell me if I will get planning permission?

No. It flags known constraints such as a conservation area or listed status. The council decides permission.

Is it available now?

No. It is planned. Early access will start with a small group of named users.
