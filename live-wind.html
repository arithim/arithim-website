Questions

Why can the live Great Britain figure look low?

The live figure counts wind farms metered on the transmission network. Several gigawatts of smaller farms are connected to the local distribution networks, and they appear only in NESO's embedded wind forecast, which the chart shows separately.

Where do the numbers come from?

Elexon publishes half-hourly generation by fuel and actual generation by type for Great Britain, and NESO publishes the wind forecasts. Other grids come from their own system operators, and the yearly figures from Ember.

How often is it updated?

Every hour. Elexon's onshore and offshore split runs about three hours behind; the other GB figures are within the last half hour.
