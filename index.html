HOW AN AGENT WORKS

One job, five steps.

Every agent we build follows the same shape, whether it runs in your environment or on Arithim hosting.

01
Start
A schedule, a file or a message starts the run.
02
Gather
It pulls what it needs from your systems.
03
Work
An AI model works through the task, within your rules.
04
Check
Anything it is unsure about goes to a person.
05
Deliver
The result lands where your team already works.
