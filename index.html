SERVICE 01 · CONVERGE

AI Readiness & Strategy

Describe this animation

Four workflows are scored for data readiness: invoice matching 82, customer enquiries 71, contract review 55 and demand planning 34. A ranked shortlist follows, with invoice matching to build first and customer enquiries next, each with data readiness, effort and risk. A note flags that demand planning has incomplete history and needs its data fixed first. All names and figures are samples.

We map every workflow and score data readiness, then converge it into one ranked, buildable target architecture.

SERVICE 02 · HUB

AI Systems & Automation

Describe this animation

An invoice PDF arrives. Four fields are extracted: supplier, purchase order number, amount of £12,480 and payment terms. Three checks run: the purchase order is found, the supplier bank details match, and the amount is £480 over the order. Because of that difference the invoice is sent to the buyer for approval with the evidence attached. All names and figures are samples.

Documents and knowledge flow into a single intelligence hub, then back out as copilots, dashboards, and automations.

SERVICE 03 · STACK

SAP ECC Intelligence Layer

Describe this animation

A read-only extract from SAP ECC, with the core unchanged. A user asks for open purchase orders past due, by plant. Tiles show 846 open orders, 57 past due across 4 plants, and bars split the 57 by plant. An exception is flagged: goods received with no invoice, 41 days old, at Plant 1000. All figures are samples.

We stack an intelligence layer on your existing SAP ECC, so signals rise into insight without ever touching the core.

SERVICE 04 · TREE

AI-Optimised Websites

Describe this animation

A service page is checked. Organisation, service and location entities are found, and an FAQ with no markup. A table of structured data shows Organization and Service valid and FAQPage missing, which then changes to added. Someone asks an AI assistant who builds compliance workflows in London, and the answer cites the page. All names and content are samples.

A crawler walks your semantic structure, so people, search engines, and AI systems all read it cleanly.

SERVICE 05 · ASCEND

Training & Enablement

Describe this animation

An operations team of 12 follows a five-module learning path on its own documents, with 9 people practising. Three modules are complete and two are still to do. Bars show how many people finished each module, from 12 of 12 down to 2 of 12, and a note says the next session is a hands-on one on running the system day to day. All figures are samples.

Teams climb a staged path, from the first workshop to a fully self-sufficient operating model.

SERVICE 06 · GATES

Regulatory & Technical Systems

Describe this animation

A change request is submitted and passes three review gates in turn: a completeness check by the system, a technical review by engineering and a compliance sign-off by the QA lead. Each step is written to an audit log with its time. The request is then released with an evidence pack of three records. All names and figures are samples.

Every action passes through explicit compliance gates, capturing evidence at each checkpoint.
