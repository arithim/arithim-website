Find Me A Job

An AI job-search product that does the searching for you. It reads a candidate's profile, matches it against live listings across the market and returns the roles worth applying to.

TYPE
Owned product
DOMAIN
Careers & recruitment AI
STATUS
Live
THE PROBLEM

Job seekers drown in near-duplicate listings spread across dozens of boards. Search is keyword-shallow, relevance is low, and the effort of filtering good roles from noise falls entirely on the candidate. Most people give up long before they run out of suitable jobs.

HOW IT WORKS

The candidate describes themselves once. A matching engine reads live listings, scores each against the profile for fit and realistic chance, ranks what's left and hands back a short list instead of another endless feed.

WHY IT MATTERS

Find Me A Job turns a demoralising scroll into a short list of roles a person can actually act on. It's a working example of Arithim's thesis: AI is most useful when it removes effort from a decision people already have to make.

"Fewer, better matches beat an infinite feed every time."
