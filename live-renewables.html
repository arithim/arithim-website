Questions

What counts as renewable here?

Solar, wind, hydro (not pumped storage), bioenergy and other renewables such as geothermal. Nuclear is not counted as renewable, and neither are imports.

Why do some grids show no renewable share?

A share needs the whole generation mix. Great Britain publishes it; for several other grids only solar and wind are available live, so the page shows their output without a share.

Where do the Great Britain numbers come from?

Solar is Sheffield Solar's PV_Live estimate; every other source is Elexon's half-hourly generation by fuel, which covers the transmission network.
