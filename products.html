PRODUCTS

What software does Arithim make?

Four products, which we build, host and support ourselves. Two are for businesses: chemical and ingredient distributors, and teams developing solar farms in Great Britain. The other two help people find a job and get their application past the first screen.

FOR CHEMICAL AND INGREDIENT DISTRIBUTORS

Technical Sales Guide

Demos and pilots

A salesperson asks for a product by application and condition, such as heat stress in broilers in Saudi Arabia. The Guide ranks products from every supplier in its catalogue and puts the dose, cost in use, landed cost and country registration beside each one, with the source.

  • A catalogue of 466 products from 147 suppliers across 8 industries.
  • Registrations tracked by product and country, with a reminder before each expiry.
  • An interface in ten languages, Arabic among them.

Describe this animation

A competitor equivalent is found and Replace swaps Principal A for Principal C, saving $0.40 per tonne of feed. All figures are samples.

FOR SOLAR DEVELOPERS IN GREAT BRITAIN

Site Finder

Demos

An origination team describes the solar farm it wants to build. Site Finder ranks candidate land across England, Scotland and Wales, and each site opens into its evidence: the grid connection, the planning record nearby and the economics.

  • Built on open public data, and the app lists the source of every input.
  • Change an assumption and the list re-ranks, with a note of which sites moved and why.
  • A one-page evidence pack for any site, ready to take to a land agent.
How Site Finder worksBook a demo
Describe this animation

A user types a brief for a 50 MW solar farm with a grid connection by 2030 in England and Wales. Three candidate sites appear in ranked order with the distance to a substation, whether headroom is published and how nearby schemes fared at planning. The user marks a substation as full, and a What changed note shows Site 31 moving to first place and Site 14 dropping to third. All names and figures are samples.

FOR PEOPLE LOOKING FOR WORK

Find Me A Job

Live

A candidate describes themselves once. Find Me A Job reads live listings, scores each one against the profile for fit and for a realistic chance of success, and hands back a short list of roles worth applying to.

  • One profile in place of a search on every job board.
  • Near-duplicate listings are dropped before the candidate sees them.

Describe this animation

A candidate types a short profile: data analyst, four years, SQL and Python, Manchester or remote. Counters show 1,240 listings read, 312 duplicates dropped and 3 roles worth a look. Three roles appear in ranked order with a fit score, the chance of success and when each was posted. All figures are samples.

FOR PEOPLE APPLYING FOR JOBS

Unrejectable

Live

A candidate adds a CV and the role they are applying for. Unrejectable reads them the way a first-pass screener would, lists the fixable reasons the application could be turned down and helps rewrite those parts before anyone at the employer sees it.

  • Each weak spot is explained against the role you entered.
  • It is the companion to Find Me A Job: one finds the roles, the other works on the application.
