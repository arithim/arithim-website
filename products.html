FOR UK FIRMS THAT HAVE TO NAME THEIR AI MODELS

Assay

Early access

Assay lists the AI models a company uses: the models its applications call over an API and the open-weight models on its own GPUs. It flags any you have ruled out and produces a dated attestation report that states its scope, method and confidence.

  • Reads logs, cloud records and code that you already have, with read-only access and metadata only.
  • Compares several sources and gives each finding a confidence level and its evidence.
  • Lists what it could not see, with the signal that partly covers each gap.
Book a scoping call →How it works

What a report holds

Each application or host, what we saw, the model family and where it came from, a result of approved, review, flagged or open, and a confidence level. The report names its scope, its date and the registry version it used.

FOR PEOPLE LOOKING FOR WORK

Find Me A Job

Live

A candidate describes themselves once. Find Me A Job reads live listings, scores each one against the profile for fit and for a realistic chance of success, and hands back a short list of roles worth applying to.

  • One profile in place of a search on every job board.
  • Near-duplicate listings are dropped before the candidate sees them.

FOR PEOPLE APPLYING FOR JOBS

Unrejectable

Live

A candidate adds a CV and the role they are applying for. Unrejectable reads them the way a first-pass screener would, lists the fixable reasons the application could be turned down and helps rewrite those parts before anyone at the employer sees it.

  • Each weak spot is explained against the role you entered.
  • It is the companion to Find Me A Job: one finds the roles, the other works on the application.

QUESTIONS

Questions about Arithim's products

Can we try a product before we commit?

For the Technical Sales Guide we run an eight-week pilot on one division in one country, with your principals' products. For Site Finder we run a demo on a project brief you choose. For Assay we start with a scoping call. Find Me A Job and Unrejectable are live at findmeajob.uk and unrejectable.uk.

What is the difference between the enterprise and SaaS products?

We set up an enterprise product for each customer, load their data and agree the scope with them, starting with a demo or a pilot. A SaaS product is the same for everyone, and you sign up on its website.

Does Arithim build software for clients too?

Yes. We also take on AI readiness work, automation, SAP ECC reporting, websites, training and regulatory systems for clients. The services page has the details.

Want an enterprise product on your own data?

Tell us which product and a little about your business, and we will set up a demo with your products, markets or project brief. The SaaS products need no call: sign up on their sites.
